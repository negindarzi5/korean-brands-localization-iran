The Role of Marketing Strategy Localization in the Market Position of Korean Brands in the Iranian Market

A Comparative Case Study of Samsung and LG

Author: Negin Darzi
B.A. Business Administration, Shomal University, Iran
Independent Undergraduate Research Project · 2026

---

About the Research

This qualitative comparative case study explores the role of marketing strategy localization in the market position of Korean brands in the Iranian market, focusing on Samsung and LG.

The research examines how adaptation to the local market may influence consumer perceptions, brand image, brand equity, and overall market positioning.

Methodology

- Literature Review
- Documentary Analysis
- Comparative Case Study
- Secondary Data Analysis

No primary surveys, interviews, questionnaires, or experiments were conducted.

Research Focus

International Marketing · Marketing Localization · Korean Brands · Consumer Behavior · Brand Image · Brand Equity · Iranian Market

Research Question

«How does marketing strategy localization contribute to the market position of Korean brands in the Iranian market?»
